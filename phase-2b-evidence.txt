3. THE DEFECT §N.8 FOUND (the reason the blind list earned its place)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
What: a command that backgrounds a process inheriting its output pipes
      returns at once, but the survivor holds the pipe write end open. The
      runner joined its two reader threads, so it waited on the SURVIVOR's
      lifetime, not the command's — past its own time limit, indefinitely.

Observed BEFORE the fix (bash /tmp/probe-n.sh, outer guard 12s):
  $ atrium-shell run --root $ROOT --cwd /home/work --timeout-ms 2000 -- sh -c '(sleep 30) &'
  observed: rc=124   elapsed=12002ms
  verdict : HUNG — exceeded the 12s outer guard.

Observed AFTER the fix (same command):
  observed: rc=0   elapsed=222ms
  output  : OK status=exit 0 stdout=0 bytes stderr=0 bytes

Fix: do not block on the readers. Poll is_finished() for a 200ms grace
(READER_GRACE in src/lib.rs), then report what was captured and return. In the
ordinary case (child exited, pipes at EOF) is_finished() is already true and
nothing is delayed. The reader threads are detached, not killed.

The survivor is NOT killed — deliberate, and documented in README.md (a process
group kill is containment, which is §2e). Observed: `sleep 30` still alive after
the runner returned.
